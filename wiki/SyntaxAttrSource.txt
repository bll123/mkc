### .source (attribute)

    .source 'file.c' ['file2.c' ...];

The __.source__ attribute is used with a
[.executable](SyntaxStmtExecutable) statement, a
[.create_file](SyntaxStmtCreateFile) or with an
[.library](SyntaxStmtLibrary) statement.

Repeatable: __yes__
