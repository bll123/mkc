### Syntax: .create_file

__.create_file__ creates a file or files given a list of commands to run.

    .create_file <file-name> [<file-name> ...] { <attribute-list> };

##### Attributes

* [.command](SyntaxAttrCommand)

##### Examples

    .create_file grammar.c grammar.h {
      .command bison -o grammar.c --defines=grammar.h grammar.y;
    }
