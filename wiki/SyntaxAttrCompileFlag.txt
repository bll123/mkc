### .compiler_flags (attribute)

    .compiler_flags '-flag' ['-flag' ...];

Note that if you use `'-I some/path'`, each individual argument must be
quoted: `'-I' 'some/path'`.  Or use `'-Isome/path'`.

The __.compiler\_flags__ attribute is used with a [check](SyntaxCheck)
statement, an [.alternate](SyntaxAttrAlternate) attribute or
with an [.executable](SyntaxStmtExecutable) statement.

The flags specified will be added to the check.

Repeatable: __yes__
