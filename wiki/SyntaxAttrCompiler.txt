### .compiler (attribute)

    .compiler <compiler-name>;

The _.compiler_ attribute is used for [.project](SyntaxStmtProject)
and [.profile](SyntaxStmtProfile) statements.

In a [.project](SyntaxStmtProject) statement, the default compiler will be
set to _compiler-name_.  This is the compiler used for compiler and
link flag checks.

In a [.profile](SyntaxStmtProfile) statement, the compiler is set
for the following statements in the profile statement block.

As of 2026-6-18, the compiler names that are recognized are 'c',
'c++', 'objc', 'bison' and 'flex'.

Repeatable: __no__
