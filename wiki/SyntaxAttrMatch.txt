### .match (attribute)

The __.match__ is used for
[.check_include_compile](SyntaxStmtCheckIncCompile),
[.check_include_dependencies](SyntaxStmtCheckIncDeps) and
[.check_include_guards](SyntaxStmtCheckIncGuards).

    .match <regular-expression>;

The __.match__ regular expression is compared against the files in the
project's include directories.  All files that match are used for the
check include statement.

Repeatable: __no__

##### Examples

    match '\.h$';
