### .method (attribute)

The __.method__ attribute is used to specify the processing method. It is
used in the [.configuration_file](SyntaxStmtConfigure) statement.

    method <method>;

For a [.configuration_file](SyntaxStmtConfigure) statement, _method_
may be one of _auto_ or _manual_.

Repeatable: __no__
