### Syntax: .auto_build

__.auto_build__ builds all executables and libraries that have been
defined.

    .auto_build;

### Syntax: .build

__.build__ builds the specified executables and libraries.

    .build <name> [<name> ...];

##### Examples

    .build 'helloworld';

    .auto_build;
