### .libraries (attribute)

The __.libraries__ attribute is used for a [check](SyntaxCheck)
statement.

    .libraries '-lname' ['-lname' ...];

The __.libraries__ attribute is used with a [check](SyntaxCheck)
statement, an [.alternate](SyntaxAttrAlternate) attribute or
with an [.executable](SyntaxStmtExecutable) statement.

The flags specified will be added to the [check](SyntaxCheck).

Repeatable: __yes__
