### .link_flags (attribute)

The __.link\_flags__ attribute is used for a [check](SyntaxCheck)
statement.

    .link_flags '-flag' ['-flag' ...];

The __.link\_flags__ attribute is used with a [check](SyntaxCheck)
statement, an [.alternate](SyntaxAttrAlternate) attribute or
with an [.executable](SyntaxStmtExecutable) statement.

The flags specified will be added to the [check](SyntaxCheck).

Repeatable: __yes__
