### .command (attribute)

    .command <command-line>;

The __.command__ attribute is used with a
[.create_file](SyntaxStmtCreateFile) statement.

The tokens in _command-line_ do not need to be quoted.

Repeatable: __yes__

##### Examples

    .command bison -o grammar.c --defines=grammar.h grammar.y;
    .command cp -fp a.txt b.txt;
