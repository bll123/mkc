### .on_success, .on_failure (attribute)

The __.on_success__ or __.on_failure__ attribute specifies a block of
statements to run on success or failure of a [check](SyntaxCheck). It
is used in all [check](SyntaxCheck) statements.

    .on_success { <statement-list> }
    .on_failure { <statement-list> }

Repeatable: __no__
